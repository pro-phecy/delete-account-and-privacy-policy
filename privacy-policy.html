This policy explains what information the Jot app ("Jot", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Jot is provided by YOUR NAME OR COMPANY. Contact: YOUR@EMAIL.

InformationWhy

We do not sell your information. We do not show ads. We do not use your notes or tasks for advertising or to train AI models.

Voice recording and transcription

When you record or upload audio, it is converted to text on your phone using a speech model that runs on the device. The audio is not uploaded to our servers. Only the resulting text is saved, as a note, if you keep it. The first time you use this feature the app downloads the speech model file from Hugging Face, which can see your IP address as part of that download.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Microphone: to record voice notes.
  • Notifications and alarms & reminders: to deliver task reminders at the time you choose. Reminders are scheduled on your phone.
  • Files: only when you choose an audio or video file to transcribe.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in system, and sends account emails such as password resets.
  • Google provides Google sign-in, if you choose it.
  • Hugging Face serves the speech model file download.

These providers process data on our behalf or, in the case of Google sign-in, under their own privacy policies. We share information only with them and only as needed to run the app, or if the law requires it.

Storage, security and offline use

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access rules ensure each account can read only its own notes and tasks. To work offline, Jot keeps a copy of your data and any unsent changes on your phone; this copy is removed when you sign out. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account in the app (Profile, then Delete account) or by following the steps at https://YOUR-SITE/delete-account. Deleting your account permanently removes your account, profile, notes and tasks. Backup copies held by our hosting provider may remain for a limited time before they expire.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your information, and to object to certain processing. You can edit or delete your notes and tasks in the app at any time. For anything else, email us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

Jot is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
