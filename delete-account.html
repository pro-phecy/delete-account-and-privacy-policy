Delete your Jot account

Jot · YOUR NAME OR COMPANY

Option 1: delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Jot and sign in.
  2. Tap your avatar at the top right of Home to open Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm.

Your account is deleted right away.

Option 2: ask us to delete it

If you can't open the app, email YOUR@EMAIL from the address you used to sign up, with the subject "Delete my Jot account". We may ask you to confirm it's you. We will delete your account within 30 days and let you know when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details
  • Your profile (name and preferences)
  • All your notes, voice transcripts and tasks, including schedules

Voice audio is never stored on our servers, so there is no audio to delete. Data saved on your phone is removed when you sign out or uninstall the app.

What may remain

Backup copies held by our hosting provider may remain for a limited time before they expire. We keep no other copy of your content.

If you signed in with Google

Deleting your Jot account does not delete your Google account. To also remove Jot's access, go to your Google Account, then Security, then Third-party access, and remove Jot.
